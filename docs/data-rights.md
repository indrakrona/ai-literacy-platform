# Data rights (UU PDP) and school reports

## What people can do

| Who | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Any user | Account page › My data | Download everything held about them (JSON), immediately. Logged as an access request. |
| Any user | Account page › My data | Ask for their account and data to be deleted; cancel while it is open. |
| Parent (account) | Parent space, per child | Withdraw permission; ask for the child's data to be deleted. |
| Parent (no account) | The original consent link | Withdraw permission. |
| Super admin | Operations › Data requests | Erase or reject (with a reason) each deletion request before its due date. |

Withdrawing permission sets the child to "declined": lessons stay readable; AI Lab, projects and tests stop.

## What erasing does

- Deleted: AI conversations, notifications, lesson progress, XP, badges, test bookings, solo projects.
- Team projects stay for teammates, without the person.
- Test attempts stay for anonymous statistics, with answers, integrity log and incidents removed.
- Certificates are revoked, so a shared verification link shows "revoked".
- Parent-consent records keep only the decision and its date (guardian contact details removed).
- The account becomes "Pengguna dihapus" with an unusable email and password, and is suspended.
- Every step is in the audit log (counts only).

## Deadlines — check with legal counsel

The due date is set in Platform settings › "Days to complete a data deletion request" (default **3 days**).
Confirm the right periods under UU PDP No. 27/2022 and its implementing regulations during the legal review.

## School report and the KKA mapping

`/sekolah/laporan` shows aggregate results by grade, class and big idea, AILIS averages, and coverage of
KKA (Koding dan Kecerdasan Artifisial) learning outcomes. It contains no individual student data and can be
downloaded as CSV or printed to PDF.

The KKA outcomes and their mapping (CMS › Learning › KKA outcomes) are a **draft** written from the
programme's lesson list. A KKA teacher must check each row against the official Capaian Pembelajaran
(wording, phase, element, mapped can-do statements), then set it to "Checked" with their name. Reports show
"draft" until every row in use is checked.
